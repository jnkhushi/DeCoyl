How it works — a closed loop

One engine protects the inbox, trains the staff, and gets sharper every round. Every attack that slips past the defender becomes training data.

01

Attacker AI

Writes realistic phishing and spear-phishing tests (synthetic, sandboxed).

02

Defender

Four layers — language, link & sender forensics, spear-phish cues, explain.

03

Misses retrain

Attacks that evade the defender feed back in; the model retrains and improves.

04

Coach staff

Safe simulations with instant coaching; each person gets a human-risk score.
